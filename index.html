SITE LAUNCH: CHAPTER 1 OF FLOURISH

Welcome to the official launch of Flourish!
Flourish is a Modern Fantasy Webcomic ARG that has been written, drawn, designed and created by me. "but who are you?" i hear you asking, so allow me to introduce myself. My name is Rook and I am your entertainment for the evening, o' heavy is the head that doth wear the stupid jingly hat. Now that we are aquainted let me set some expectations. In order to play this game and/or read this comic, i am in need of a couple things to make the game's save-states work properly. namely your Email and a password of your choosing. these will never be shared with external entities and are strictly for game purposes only. that being said i hope you enjoy the first chapter of Flourish, it was a huge labor of love from not only myself but so many amazing and wonderful artists, please check all of them out through the gallery, all of their information should be available from there.
